I'm interested in the intersection of technology and business – especially the parts of tech companies that rarely show up in architecture diagrams: how knowledge is shared, how teams are structured and how people grow.

This blog is where I think out loud about those topics – knowledge management, organizational structures and people development – from both a technical and a business perspective. Expect observations from practice, half-finished ideas and the occasional strong opinion.

The blog takes its name from the philosopher Michael Polanyi: *"We know more than we can tell."* Much of what makes organizations work lives in people's heads and habits rather than in documents. Making some of that visible is the goal here.

Posts appear irregularly. If these topics interest you, check back now and then or subscribe via RSS.
